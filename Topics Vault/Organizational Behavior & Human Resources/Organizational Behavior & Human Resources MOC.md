# Organizational Behavior & Human Resources

Diversity, discrimination, emotions and moods, perception and decision making, and motivation at work, from OBHR 330. Heavily cross-linked with the Business Law employment chapters, since Title VII/ADEA/ADA are the legal side of the same material. Chapters 6 and 7 also connect to Business Ethics (Wells Fargo's sales quotas show up as the textbook example of reward systems and goal setting driving unethical behavior) and to ECON 301 (sunk cost fallacy vs escalation of commitment, incentives).
